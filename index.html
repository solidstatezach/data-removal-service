    Straight answers

    Can you actually remove my data, or just hide it?

    Remove it at the source. We file formal opt-outs and deletion requests with each broker, and for California residents we also file through the state's DROP system, which legally requires registered brokers to delete your personal information. Search-result removals from Google are filed as a second layer.

    Why do listings come back after removal?

    Brokers buy fresh data feeds constantly — voter rolls, utility hookups, warranty cards, other brokers. A removed listing can be rebuilt from a new feed within months. That's exactly why verification and ongoing re-checks matter more than the first opt-out.

    What do you need from me?

    Your name (including past names and common variations), current and past cities, and an email address for broker verifications. Some brokers ask for ID confirmation — when one does, we'll tell you exactly what's needed and why before anything is sent.

    How long does a full scrub take?

    Most opt-outs are filed within the first week. Brokers take anywhere from a few days to several weeks to process deletions, and we verify each one rather than trusting the confirmation email. Expect a complete verified report in about 30 days.

    Can I just do this myself?

    Yes — honestly. The plan builder above gives you the real sequence for free. What you're paying for here is the hours: dozens of different forms, verification loops, follow-ups, and re-checks. If you'd rather spend a weekend on it, the plan will get you there.
